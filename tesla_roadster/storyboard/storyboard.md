# Storyboard: Tesla Roadster, the technology behind an electric supercar

**Takeaway:** a battery, an inverter and a motor form one loop that runs forwards to accelerate and backwards to brake.
**Key reveal / number:** 0-60 mph in 3.9 s (with 6,831 cells and 53 kWh as the battery numbers).
**Presenter's arc:** surprised -> happy -> thinking -> happy (thumbs up, waves goodbye).
**Palette progression:** sunset road (story) -> violet (battery science) -> navy circuit -> deep violet motor -> cream chart -> orange speedo -> dusk road (regen) -> pink sunset road (finale).
**Assumed / conceptual:** torque curves are conceptual shapes (tagged CONCEPT), not measured data. Component positions in the x-ray car are approximate. Dot matrix behind 6,831 is texture, not one dot per cell.
